# Design System — Version 0.1

## Direction
Modern Classic × Dark Academia × Minimal Vintage × restrained Victorian elegance. Calm, editorial and highly legible; not gothic, cluttered or overly ornamental.

## Typography
- Editorial headings: Cormorant Garamond, with Georgia fallback.
- Interface/body: DM Sans, with Arial fallback.
- Data and controls remain in the sans-serif family.

## Layout
- Desktop expandable left sidebar, compact top bar and centered main content.
- Mobile drawer-style navigation and touch-friendly controls.
- Thin borders, restrained corners, generous whitespace and subtle transitions.
- Reduced-motion preference is respected.

## Department accents
Health: olive; Mind: burgundy/rose; Daily Life: brass; Home: sage; Education: muted blue; Finance: forest green; Relationships: mauve; Life Skills: slate.

## Components currently represented
Sidebar, primary navigation, department navigation, topbar, dashboard panels, department list, empty states, theme switch, search dialog, capture dialog, toast and mobile navigation toggle.

## Review status
Initial direction only. Contrast and keyboard/accessibility audits remain pending. This is the first reviewable shell, not the final design system.
