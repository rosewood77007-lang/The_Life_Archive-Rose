# Roadmap

1. **Foundation (current):** reconcile requirements, project scaffold, design tokens, responsive shell, authentication, private workspace, database connection and initial security policies.
2. **Organizational engine:** departments, nested folders/pages, custom fields, tasks, projects, goals, calendar and Quick Capture persistence.
3. **Personal information:** notes, distinct Classic Diary and Modern Journal, File Vault, attachments, search, export and recovery.
4. **Eight departments:** domain-specific workflows and links to shared records.
5. **Research and media:** external research, current news, bookmarks, video library and authorized integrations.
6. **Rose Intelligence:** AI provider, consent controls, research/planning/knowledge assistance and user-approved actions.
7. **Advanced functionality:** analytics, reviews, customization, offline access, synchronization and conflict resolution.
8. **Production readiness:** security, performance, accessibility, backup/restore, deployment and documentation verification.

A stage is complete only after its functionality and relevant tests pass, not when its screens exist.
